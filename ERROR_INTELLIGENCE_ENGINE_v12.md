# Shodlik Education — v12 Error Intelligence & Personalized Remediation

## Purpose
Turn wrong answers into structured learning evidence rather than a flat mistake list.

## Flow
wrong answer → error category → concept → micro-lesson → 3-step remediation → targeted practice → successful recheck → delayed review → resolved

## Error categories
- tense-choice
- auxiliary-word-order
- form-morphology
- articles-determiners
- prepositions
- lexical-choice
- collocation
- translation-transfer
- listening-discrimination
- meaning-inference
- target-form
- spelling
- other

## Resolution rule
A mistake becomes `recovering` after one successful recheck and `resolved` after two successful rechecks. Resolved errors are excluded from targeted practice, but the last review date is retained for future spaced retrieval.

## Methodological note
The classifier is deterministic and evidence-based from exercise metadata/type/context. It is not presented as a diagnosis of the learner. Repeated response patterns are treated as evidence of a likely misconception, not proof of a cognitive cause.
