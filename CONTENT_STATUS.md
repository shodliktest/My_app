# Shodlik Education — Content Production Status v5

This pass adds a deterministic lesson-specific authoring layer on top of the existing curriculum.

## Authoring pass
- Every lesson now receives level-aware vocabulary targets, curated collocations, functional phrases, coherent reading material, conversational listening, controlled-to-free speaking scaffolding, writing scaffolding, and lesson-specific exercises.
- Content is generated from each lesson's real topic, grammar examples, level and lexical set rather than generic placeholders.
- Existing authored material is preserved and extended.
- Collocations are selected from curated domain lists rather than invented per word.
- Generated content is explicitly not presented as externally textbook-certified until human editorial review is completed.

## Pedagogical progression
- Pre-A1/A1: recognition, controlled production, survival English.
- A2: everyday communication, short reasons and descriptions.
- B1/B1+: connected speech, opinions, explanations, problem solving.
- B2/B2+: complex grammar, argumentation, nuance, register.
- C1: precision, synthesis, qualification, academic/professional register.
