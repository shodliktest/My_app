# Teacher Content QA — v5

## Automated quality gates
- No duplicate lesson IDs.
- No known placeholder phrases such as `namunaviy gap`, `use word`, or `common word`.
- Every lesson has grammar, vocabulary, phrases, listening, reading, speaking, writing, exercises and a test.
- Every lesson receives level-aware vocabulary/phrase targets and controlled-to-free speaking/writing scaffolding.
- Vocabulary collocations are selected from curated domain banks; empty lexical relationships are not fabricated.

## Editorial quality gates still required before final commercial release
- Human review of every reading/listening script.
- CEFR calibration of every item.
- Distractor quality review for every MCQ.
- Native-speaker review of idiomaticity and register at B2+/C1.
- Audio recording and pronunciation review.
