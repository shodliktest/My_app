73. AGENTGA BERILADIGAN ASOSIY TOPSHIRIQ

Agentga “English app yasab ber” desangiz, u oddiy app chiqarib qo‘yishi mumkin.

Buning o‘rniga unga PRD sifatida quyidagi talabni berish kerak:

> Build an adaptive CEFR-aligned English learning platform from Pre-A1 to C1.

The application must integrate grammar, vocabulary, active phrases, listening, reading, speaking, writing, pronunciation, spaced repetition, error correction, adaptive learning, games, flashcards, daily missions, progress tracking and AI tutoring.

Each daily lesson must connect one theme across all skills rather than teaching isolated grammar.

Every exercise must provide immediate feedback, explanation, Uzbek support when required, and a second attempt.

The system must maintain a personal error bank and automatically generate targeted revision.

Vocabulary must support image, audio, definition, translation, example, collocations, word family, synonyms, antonyms, active/passive status and spaced repetition.

The flashcard system must support active recall and multiple directions: image→word, word→meaning, Uzbek→English, English→Uzbek, audio→word, sentence completion and speaking prompts.

Listening must support transcript, subtitles, adjustable playback speed, comprehension questions, dictation and shadowing.

Reading must support clickable vocabulary, comprehension, inference, summary and speaking/writing follow-up tasks.

Speaking must support AI conversation, pronunciation analysis, fluency feedback and grammar correction.

Writing must support AI correction, explanation, rewriting and progressive difficulty.

The curriculum must be divided into Pre-A1, A1, A2, B1, B1+, B2, B2+ and C1.

The system must use CEFR descriptors as the curriculum framework and map each lesson and assessment to specific skills and outcomes.

The platform must support offline lessons and synchronized cloud progress.

Include learner dashboard, teacher/admin dashboard, content management, AI content generation, approval workflow, analytics, assessment engine and adaptive learning engine.

The UI must be modern, fast, responsive, accessible and optimized for Android APK and web.

Do not build a static collection of pages. Build a real learning engine where learner performance dynamically changes review, practice and lesson recommendations.
