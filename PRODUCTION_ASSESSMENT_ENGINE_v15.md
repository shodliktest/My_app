# Shodlik Education v15 — Speaking & Writing Professional Assessment Engine

## Purpose
Local-first CEFR assessment for speaking and writing. It does not require an AI API.

## Rubric
- Task achievement
- Grammar range/control
- Vocabulary
- Coherence
- Writing accuracy
- Speaking fluency signal

## Pedagogy
1. Give a CEFR-appropriate task.
2. Produce a deterministic diagnostic.
3. Show evidence rather than an opaque score.
4. Identify strengths and priorities.
5. Generate a retry task targeting the weakest rubric.
6. Record speaking/writing skill progress in the existing mastery system.

## Limitations
- Browser speech recognition quality depends on the device/browser.
- Pronunciation cannot be reliably scored from transcript text alone; v15 explicitly labels this as a limitation.
- Heuristic assessment is not a substitute for a certified human CEFR examiner.
- AI can optionally enhance feedback later, but the local engine remains functional without it.
